Doors & finishes

Sliding doors save space and can make a room look bigger. Pick a frame, a panel and a track, then choose the finish for the inside of the robe.

Sliding doors in three choices

Sliding doors carry a 2 year warranty. Hinged doors, bifolds and jamb and mullion framing are available too.

1 Frame

Framed, or semi-frameless (mirrorline) for a cleaner edge.

Frames in white, brushed aluminium, polished aluminium or black. Ask about other colours.

2 Panel

Mirror
Adds light and makes the room feel bigger.
White glass
A brilliant super-white finish.
Translucent glass
Soft light through, with a slight green tint.
Painted glass
Any colour, to match your décor.
Printed glass
Your own photo printed life size, in full colour.
White melamine
Classic textured white to match your robe.
Vinyl
Vinyl-coated board to match the frame colour.
Raw gyprock
Supplied ready to paint any colour you like.

3 Track

Double or triple track, or our slimline and softline systems. The right one depends on how many doors you have and how much of the robe you need open at once.

Polytec Surano

Shaker-style sliding doors with a 130mm rail, available in a range of colours.

Colours & finishes

We use board from Polytec, Laminex, Formica and Lamicolor. Lighter colours brighten a small robe; timbergrain makes a large one feel luxurious.

  • White melamine

    Our standard. Clean, bright and the fastest to install.

  • Edged melamine

    White board with a brushed silver or timbergrain edge for a touch of detail.

  • Timbergrain

    Warm woodgrain boards for a premium, built-in look. Allow up to 3 weeks.
