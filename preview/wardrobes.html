Wardrobes

Every robe we build is designed for your room and your things. Choose the type of robe, then the system that suits your budget, then the finish.

Walk-in robes

Fit out a walk-in with our standard system, or step up to our luxury cabinetry range. Either way the layout is planned around what you own: how much long and double hanging, how many drawers, where the shoes go.

  • Classic white or timbergrain finishes
  • Shelving up to the ceiling to use every bit of height
  • Room for a centre buffet in larger spaces
See walk-in robes we’ve built

Built-in robes

Replace the single shelf and rail in an existing robe, or add a full built-in robe to a room that has none. Pair it with sliding or hinged doors to finish it off.

  • Internals only, or a complete robe with doors
  • Works in old homes where nothing is square
  • Linen, pantry and garage shelving too
See built-in robes we’ve built

Boutique robes

For a dressing room that feels like a boutique. Boutique robes get an exclusive consultation with detailed plans, concept development and colour advice, and the highest quality finishes we offer.

Add a centre buffet as the focal point: a glass top to show off jewellery and watches, velvet-lined trays, and melamine or perspex dividers.
