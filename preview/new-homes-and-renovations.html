Tips for a new build

  1. Check your builder’s specs

    Most project builders include just one shelf and a hanging rail in robes, and limited shelving in the linen.

  2. Ask for the robes to be left empty

    If you plan to upgrade, have your builder leave the space bare. Otherwise you may pay for rip-out, dumping, patching and painting.

  3. Install before the flooring

    In most cases the robe goes in before flooring, so you can change floors later and leave the robe untouched.

  4. Paint the walls first

    Walls should be painted before installation. A contrasting colour can make your robes really stand out.

  5. Expect a final measure

    Dimensions often change once walls and plasterboard are done, so we always measure again before installing.

  6. Install before handover

    With your builder’s OK, we can install before you get the keys.
