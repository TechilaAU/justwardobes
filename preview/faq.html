Cost & timing

How much does a custom wardrobe cost?

Every robe is made to fit your space, so the price depends on size, system and finish. The measure, design and quote are free with no obligation, and we work to your budget. Suspended systems are our most budget-friendly option.

How long does installation take after I order?

Standard white melamine or silver-edge melamine is usually installed within 1 week. Timbergrain finishes can take up to 3 weeks, depending on our schedule.

How do I pay?

Payment in full is due on the day of installation by bank transfer, cash or cheque. Larger jobs may need a 50% deposit. Funds must clear before installation.

What warranty do I get?

7 years on installation (structural integrity), 7 years on componentry and 2 years on sliding doors.

Design

What colours can I choose?

White is standard. You can upgrade to edged melamine or a full timbergrain finish from Polytec, Laminex, Formica and Lamicolor ranges to match your home.

How deep and tall is your shelving?

Standard depth is 300 to 450mm. Height is set to suit your robe, usually between 2040 and 2100mm for our standard system.

Is your system modular?

Our standard system is made to measure and fixed directly to your walls with channel and bracket componentry. We can build in modular units if you prefer.

What are your drawers made of?

Metal-sided drawers with a 16mm melamine back and base. Drawer fronts match the rest of your robe.

How do I get the most out of a small wardrobe?

Add double hanging where you can and run shelving up to the ceiling. A good layout makes a small robe hold far more than it looks like it should.

How much hanging space should I allow?

Look at what you hang now and leave room for what you will buy later. We will talk through it at the measure.

Installation

Can I install it myself?

You can, but our long-serving installers know our systems inside out, so installation is quick and pain free.

Do you do linen cupboards and pantries?

Yes. We supply and install shelving for any room in the home, plus garages, offices and commercial spaces.

What areas do you service?

From Lennox Head in the south to the Sunshine Coast in the north, including Brisbane, the Gold Coast and some western areas.

Do you work with asbestos?

No. We are not licensed to handle asbestos, so we can’t take on jobs where it is present.
