How it works

One team from first call to final clean up. You deal with real people who turn up when they say they will.

  1. 1

    Get in touch

    Call us or send the quote form with a few details about your space. Have house plans? Email them and we can design straight from those.

  2. 2

    Free measure

    A designer visits your home or site to measure up, look at how you store things and talk through options. No pressure, no obligation.

  3. 3

    Plans & quote

    You get detailed plans, wall elevations and 3D views so you know exactly what you’re getting, along with a fixed quote. Change it as much as you need.

  4. 4

    Made & installed

    We manufacture locally and install on a day that suits you, usually within a week for white melamine. We arrive on time and clean up after ourselves.

Small enough to care, big enough to deliver
